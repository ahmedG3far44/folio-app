# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Portfolio owners — developers, designers, and creatives who need a personal website to showcase their work, experience, and skills. They manage their content through profile management pages that should feel as polished as the public-facing portfolio itself.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio builder that lets users create, customize, and publish a personal portfolio site. The management dashboard is an extension of the brand — not a generic admin panel.

## Brand Personality

Bold, minimal, sharp. Confident whitespace, deliberate typography, restrained color. The interface earns trust through precision, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- No LinkedIn-clone experience cards (standard logo + title + date rows)
- No generic SaaS dashboard templates (sidebar-heavy admin panel feel)
- No glassmorphism, gradient text, or decorative blurs
- No hero-metric layouts (big numbers, small labels)

## Design Principles

1. **The page IS the portfolio** — management screens aren't a separate "admin" experience. They carry the same visual language as the public site.
2. **Confidence through restraint** — bold means saying more with less. Every element earns its place.
3. **Structure before decoration** — strong typography hierarchy and spacing do the work. Avoid surface-level embellishments.
4. **Cards are not containers** — use cards only when they're the right affordance, not the default wrapper. Nested cards are always wrong.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Supports dark/light themes (user-selectable theme system already in place)
